Physician & Diabetologist — Founder, Aura Hospital

Dr. Grishma Patel is a physician and diabetologist with 12 years of clinical experience in managing diabetes, thyroid disorders, and general internal medicine. She founded Aura Hospital with a simple goal: give patients the time and continuity that chronic conditions actually require.

Her approach blends evidence-based treatment with practical lifestyle guidance, helping patients understand not just what to do, but why — so changes actually stick.
