Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to fast before my diabetes checkup?
If Dr. Patel has ordered a fasting blood sugar test, you'll be told in advance. For a standard consultation, fasting isn't required — just bring your recent reports if you have them.
How do I schedule a visit?
Call the clinic directly at +91 63545 31905 during OPD hours, and our team will help you find a convenient slot with Dr. Patel.
Does Aura Hospital accept insurance?
We accept most major insurance plans. Call the clinic at +91 63545 31905 to confirm your specific provider before your visit.
Can I get a follow-up consultation over the phone?
Yes — for established patients with stable conditions, Dr. Patel offers phone follow-ups for routine dose checks. Ask about this when booking.
